## Route accuracy

RouteMate’s local optimizer uses approximate straight-line distance. Its field-day forecast uses the travel buffer entered by the user and is not a live ETA. It does not account for live traffic, road closures, legal restrictions, tolls, borders, vehicle dimensions, or road conditions. Users must review the final route in Google Maps and obey road signs and applicable law.
